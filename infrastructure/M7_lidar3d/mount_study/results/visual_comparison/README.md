# Visual comparison of the R1 and R4 mounts

| | Mount in base_link | Pitch | Optical centre above the top platform |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R1** (green) | (0, 0, 0.50) m | 7.5° down | 0.150 m |
| **R4** (magenta) | (0, 0, 0.40) m | 6.75° down | 0.050 m |

Both use the full 28° × 14° FoV and 4,726 rays.

Generated by `../../scripts/visual_comparison.py`.

## How the images are made

Rendering is offline (matplotlib + ffmpeg). Isaac Sim's RTX renderer cannot create a graphics device on this server.
All the geometry is the geometry Isaac Sim uses:
* **Robot:** the TB4 Standard meshes exported from the Isaac Sim USD.
* **Scene:** walls and moving obstacles from the recorded NAV03 evaluation (`ref_chase_c10`, `model_500`).
* **Returns:** the analytic ray caster, validated against Isaac Sim's ray caster in M7.
* **Robot attitude:** the measured nominal one (0.25° nose-up).

The Mirrorcle housing dimensions are not available. The sensor is drawn as a labeled 3 cm marker at the optical
centre, with a boresight arrow.

## Colours

* Ray classes: floor grey, wall blue, static obstacle orange, moving obstacle purple.
* Escaping rays: red crosses. There are none for R1 or R4.

## Files

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `1_mounting.png` | Side, front, top and isometric views of each mount on the robot. Shows the top-platform and optical-centre heights, and the FoV frustum to 0.55 m. |
| `2_scene.png` | NAV03 episode 56, step 15 (t = 3.0 s): OB1 at 0.57 m and OB2 at 1.29 m, both in view. Same robot pose and obstacles for both mounts. Rows: 3D scene with robot, obstacles, FoV frustum and point cloud; top-down; side view. |
| `3_pov.png` | The same frame from the LiDAR's point of view. Per mount: a first-person view from the optical centre (scene geometry plus returns, LiDAR FoV dashed), the range image and the ray-class image. |
| `4_animation.mp4` | The whole of episode 56 (32.6 s, 2× real time). Top: LiDAR POV. Bottom: top-down view. Scans are acquired at 4 Hz and held between acquisitions, as in the environment. |
| `selection.json` | The chosen frame, the per-mount ray counts and fractions, and the episode means. |
